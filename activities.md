## Workshops

The **HDM workshop series** (High Dimensional Data Mining) is held with the IEEE
International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM).

- **2026 · [HDM'26: 13th ICDM Workshop on High Dimensional Data Mining]({{ '/hdm26/' | relative_url }})**,
  ICDM 2026, Shenyang, China. Eight papers, with a focus on interpretable high dimensional data mining.
- **2020 · [HDM'20: High Dimensional Data Mining Workshop]({{ '/2020/03/25/hdm20-high-dimensional-data-mining-workshop/' | relative_url }})**,
  ICDM 2020.
- **2017 · [Workshop on High Dimensional Data Analysis]({{ '/2017/08/28/workshop-on-high-dimensional-data-analysis/' | relative_url }})**
  (HDM'17).
- **2014 · [HDM'14: 2nd International Workshop on High Dimensional Data Mining]({{ '/2014/05/05/call-for-submissions-2nd-international-workshop-on-high-dimensional-data-mining-hdm14/' | relative_url }})**,
  ICDM 2014.
